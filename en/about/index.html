I'm Shijie Xie. I build coding agents at MiniMax, then use them to ship my own work. Been through every shift in AI coding: prompts, context, harness. This site keeps my public work and the notes from each.

What I keep coming back to

Across several jobs, I've kept coming back to one question: how can developers get more done? From developer productivity to code intelligence to AI coding, the tools changed, and so did the calls I had to make.

These days I care most about how an agent gets the right context, how its results get verified, and how mistakes surface while it's still running.
